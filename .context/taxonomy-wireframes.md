# Taxonomy & Wireframe Blueprint

## 1. Global Taxonomy
Use these exact arrays for all navigation menus, category filters, and seller upload dropdowns:
* **Gênero:** Masculino, Feminino
* **Acessórios:** Bones, Buckets, Gorros, Balaclavas, Luvas, Meias, Cuecas, Outros
* **Sneakers:** Nike, Adidas, Balenciaga, Lanvin, Louis Vuitton
* **Roupas:** Regatas, Camisetas, Bermudas, Fleece, Moletom, Jaquetas, Calças
* **Marcas:** Supreme, Palace, Bape, Stüssy, Corteiz, Nike, Adidas, The north face, Arc’teryx, Salomon, Off-white, Palm Angels, Gucci, Louis Vuitton, Prada, Burberry, Dior, Versace, Lanvin, Balenciaga, Botega Venetta, Givenchy, Outras

## 2. Storefront Wireframes
* **Home:** Header with taxonomy dropdowns. Hero section (contains 3D animation). "Novidades" and "Por Marca" grids.
* **PLP (Product Listing Page):** Sidebar filters (Categoria, Subcategoria, Marca, Condição, Preço). Product grid showing photo, brand, name, condition, and price.
* **PDP (Product Detail Page):** Breadcrumbs, photo gallery, and a data block containing Brand, Name, Condition, Authenticity Seal, Price, Shipping Calculator, and a primary "Add to Cart" CTA. Cross-sell block at the bottom.
* **Cart:** List of items (photo, name, price, remove button), subtotal, shipping calc, total, and checkout CTA.
* **Checkout:** Multi-step route covering Identificação, Endereço, Frete, Pagamento, and Confirmação.
* **Auth:** Tabbed Login / Criar Conta com standard fields.

## 3. Seller PWA Wireframes
* **Upload/Admin:** Mobile-first form. Features a photo upload input, cascaded selects for the taxonomy (Marca, Categoria, Subcategoria), Condição, Preço, Peso/Dimensões, and a massive "Salvar e processar fotos" CTA.
